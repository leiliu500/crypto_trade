Strategy and review

Enter when the completed daily close exceeds its 40-day average by 1.6739%; exit at or below that average. Between those thresholds, retain the target. A signal becomes eligible two UTC days later, during the first minute of that day. Entry is capped at $1,000 and 10% of cash, with volume and displayed-depth limits.

Paper fees assume 0.80% per side. Open ETH is valued at a verified bid less estimated exit fees. Missing marks stay unavailable. Review requires six calendar months and at least ten completed ETH40 episodes; there is no automatic promotion to live trading.
