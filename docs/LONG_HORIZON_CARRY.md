This fixed development protocol evaluates a BTC spot purchase hedged by an equal-quantity short linear perpetual. It tests whether persistent funding can pay for retail execution, independently observed spot/perpetual basis movements, and the capital tied up in two separate balances. It is a distinct hypothesis from the concurrently registered 55-day/20-day channel trend strategy. Neither hypothesis becomes a fallback chosen after inspecting the other's result.

The numerical specification is `src/carry/horizon-spec.ts`. Its exported SHA-256, this document, input manifests, source code and registration timestamp must be sealed into the study's protocol artifact before the first outcome is calculated. A failed result stays preserved. No asset search, parameter fitting, fee-tier substitution or post-result threshold reduction is authorized by this protocol. BTC was selected using the prior funding-stability audit; that earlier inspection makes the selection developmental, not independent evidence of future performance.

The two independent account simulations cover `[2024-01-01, 2025-01-01)` and `[2025-01-01, 2025-07-01)`. They start with $100,000 paper equity and no positions. Both periods have already been available to earlier research and are reused development/confirmation windows, not untouched holdouts. Only earlier permitted source history can warm the forecast. No 2026 accrual, price, or strategy outcome is evaluated.

Use the independently retrieved Kraken spot **weekly** BTC/USD candles, preserving their source Thursday openings and 10,080-minute intervals, together with BTC/USD linear-perpetual hourly candles. The spot dataset and raw responses are separately hashed; do not derive spot from perpetual prices or index prices. The official [spot OHLC API](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/market-data/get-ohlc-data) supports weekly intervals, limits retrospective retrieval, and includes a current incomplete row. The Thursday alignment is observed in the archived source timestamps, rather than promised by that documentation. Exclude incomplete rows and reject missing, duplicate, conflicting, misaligned or malformed required observations.

Weekly OHLC is a trade aggregate, not a simultaneous executable quote. A recorded week-open price is used solely as a first-trade price proxy. Its first actual trade's intra-week timestamp, bid/ask spread, available depth and synchronization with the perpetual are not observed. Future weekly high/low/close/volume cannot be used to choose an earlier entry or its quantity. These limits remain explicit even if all arithmetic reconciles.

Every Thursday at 00:01 UTC, form the decision from already completed observations. The funding history cutoff is Thursday 00:00 UTC. A new entry resulting from that decision can occur only at the **next** source Thursday's 00:00 week-open proxy. This deliberately imposes a complete source-week delay and avoids assigning a signal calculated after 00:00 to the earlier 00:00 opening price. Reference spot value is the most recent completed source-week close, and reference perpetual value is the most recent completed hourly close. At entry, take the opening-price proxy for both legs, apply adverse slippage, quantize a common BTC quantity downward and recheck the frozen forecast's cost coverage and entry capital caps using those current price proxies. No later forecast or return can influence that opening decision.

The forecast requires exactly 2,160 consecutive matured hourly absolute funding rates from the trailing 90 days. A positive absolute rate represents USD received per BTC short for the relevant hour. Let `a` be their arithmetic mean in USD/BTC/hour. For an entry quantity `q`, the fixed 180-day, 50%-haircut funding estimate is:

```text
forecastFundingCashUsd = 0.50 × q × a × (180 × 24)
annualizedFundingFraction = a × (365 × 24) / decisionPerpetualPrice
```

The annualized fraction is a diagnostic on decision-time perpetual notional; the cash forecast uses absolute rates directly. Multiplying historical relative rates by a later price is not a substitute for exact funding cash. The haircut is a fixed research assumption, not a confidence interval or estimated probability of profit. Negative or missing expected coverage means no entry, with a recorded reason.

For the normal funding mapping, an archived normalized hour-end `e` represents `[e - 1 hour, e)` and becomes known at `e`. The alternative mapping moves that same rate to `[e, e + 1 hour)`, with known time `e + 1 hour`. Both forecast history and realized accrual use the selected mapping. This is an **effective-interval timestamp sensitivity**, not an additional publication delay. At a Thursday 00:01 decision, admit mapped ends at or before Thursday 00:00; impose no second lag. Funding cash for a held partial interval is `q × absoluteRate × heldFraction`. Missing held rates make full net P&L unknown, while any known partial cash remains separately visible. Kraken describes funding between longs and shorts and its hourly settlement/position-change mechanics in its [linear contract specifications](https://support.kraken.com/articles/4844359082772-linear-multi-collateral-derivatives-contract-specifications). Current documentation and archive overlap support the normal mapping; historical client receipt timing is not established.

The four registered scenario combinations are base/stress execution costs crossed with normal/+1-hour effective funding intervals. Each is run in both periods, giving eight required runs.

| Execution assumption, per executed side | Base | Stress |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Spot taker fee | 80 bps | 100 bps |
| Perpetual taker fee | 5 bps | 7.5 bps |
| Adverse slippage on each of the four fills | 2 bps | 5 bps |

Buy spot above the reference first-trade price and sell the perpetual below it; closing uses the opposite adverse directions. Fees apply to each actual executed notional. Do not charge the slippage allowance twice: adverse execution prices realize it in the ledger, while an explicit allowance belongs in the ex-ante entry cost test. The [current published Kraken fee schedule](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule) displays an 80-bp entry-tier spot taker fee; account tier and jurisdiction are unverified. The base assumption is not historical fee-tier evidence. The stress fee is a registered adverse scenario, not an account rate. Neither maker execution nor a more favorable tier is inferred from a limit order or the hypothetical $100,000 paper balance.

The account buys unleveraged spot with its own USD cash and separately commits collateral equal to **twice the actual perpetual entry notional**. No spot appreciation, uncommitted account cash or other asset is automatically transferred into the derivative balance. Spot cash includes its entry fee; the derivative entry fee is debited from its committed collateral. For quantity `q`, define `C` as the all-in spot purchase cash plus that committed collateral. Reject an entry that lacks either cash bucket or that violates either leg's entry notional cap `min($1,000, 1% current equity)`. Use the common 0.0001-BTC research lot and floor once; quantities below one common lot cannot enter. The fixed matched quantity is held until both legs close; no adding, averaging, independent leg trimming or automatic collateral repair is modeled. The notional cap here applies at entry; report subsequent marked notionals, which can exceed entry notional as price changes. This narrow research convention does not amend the running account's exposure limits.

The entry must satisfy the strict inequality:

```text
forecastFundingCashUsd
  > estimatedFourFillFeesAndSlippageUsd
    + 0.0075 × perpetualEntryNotionalUsd
    + 0.05 × C × 180 / 365
```

The 75-bp basis/unwind reserve is an entry allowance, not fabricated realized cash. Expected four-fill costs reserve both spot and perpetual entry and exit at current reference notionals. No convergence profit is presumed from current basis. The complete realized ledger instead reports `q × (spotExit - spotEntry)` and `q × (perpetualEntry - perpetualExit)` separately, all four actual modeled fees, and every signed funding posting. Their sum is funded cash net. The actual spot/perpetual basis change is therefore included rather than erased by a synthetic perfect hedge.

This criterion is restrictive before looking at any outcomes. For equal $1,000 reference notionals and approximately $3,000 committed capital, the base four-fill cost is **178 bps** of one-leg notional: 160 spot-fee bps, 10 perpetual-fee bps and eight slippage bps. The basis reserve adds 75 bps. The 5% annual capital hurdle over 180 days adds 739.73 bps, because it applies to roughly three times one-leg notional. After undoing the 50% forecast haircut, the trailing funding annualization must exceed approximately **40.26%**. Stress requires approximately **42.57%**. Exact fee-inclusive capital and unequal leg prices modify these figures slightly. Thus zero qualifying entries is a plausible economic result; it cannot be called a repaired trading engine or used to justify weakening the test after the result appears.

Close the pair at the first eligible spot-week opening at or after 180 elapsed days, or after two successive Thursday decisions find a strictly negative mean over exactly 672 matured funding hours (28 days), or after collateral protection requests an exit. Weekly signal exits use the next source-week opening, preserving the same causal timing. A missing funding mean is unknown, not a negative/positive or reset observation. Once an exit is requested, it remains requested. Do not reverse or re-enter on the exit's opening boundary: a fresh later weekly decision and its subsequent eligible source-week opening are required.

At every perpetual hour, conservatively mark the segregated short collateral against that hour's adverse high, including accrued/postable funding and a funded close-fee allowance. The local protection threshold is **20% of current perpetual notional**. A crossing requests pair closure at the next eligible spot-week opening. Any crossing, including during the wait for that opening, is a collateral guard breach and rejects the run. Do not assign an immediate synchronized spot exit that this dataset cannot show, grant spot collateral credit, or invent an automatic transfer. The 20% local guard is not verified historical maintenance margin or a liquidation price. Report `historicalMarginVerified: false` until authoritative period-specific venue requirements and the relevant margin marks are available; unknown historical margin blocks eligibility even when the local proxy appears solvent.

Terminal accounting closes at the last source-week opening whose full source bar ends by the study window's end. Entries are not categorically prohibited during the last 180 days, but none can execute at or after that terminal opening. This means the terminal rule can shorten a nominal 180-day trade; report entry time, intended horizon, requested/actual proxy exit time, and truncated days for every cycle. The 180-day entry forecast remains the registered decision rule; it is not silently shortened to make the truncated result look favorable. Shortened terminal episodes must not be described as evidence of a completed 180-day holding strategy. Unresolved legs at terminal time make full net P&L unknown.

For every cycle and full run, report funded cash net and the separate capital benchmark. The benchmark is the fixed **5% simple annual hurdle on actual committed capital for actual elapsed holding time**; it is not an observed yield, a risk-free-return claim, or interest credited into the cash ledger. Benchmark excess equals funded cash net minus that hurdle. Report flat cash with zero trading P&L as an additional baseline. Returns use committed spot cash plus derivative collateral, not just one leg or a fictitious net-zero cost. Unused account cash receives no invented interest. Reserve allowances are not counted as actual expenses, but actual basis losses and modeled execution costs are.

Eligibility requires all eight registered runs to have known source/funding/accounting/margin evidence, no collateral guard breaches, no unresolved legs, at least one completed cycle per period/scenario, **strictly positive funded cash net**, and **strictly positive capital-benchmark excess**. Report gross basis/price P&L, spot fees, derivative fees, funding, modeled slippage, committed capital, holding duration, terminal truncation, all blocked-entry reasons, minimum collateral buffer and peak marked exposures independently. Source gaps, unverified historical margin or a loss cannot be hidden by aggregating another scenario. A zero-trade outcome has zero trading P&L and fails the completed-cycle/profit requirements; it is not assigned a positive return or unknown profit merely because the entry hurdle was conservative.

Even positive arithmetic under this protocol cannot establish executable profitability. Historical weekly spot opens and hourly perpetual candles do not establish synchronized available quotes, two-leg fill probability, hedge-repair costs, real margin behavior or actual funding receipts. The protocol therefore hard-disables full validation, runtime activation and real orders. A separate independently recorded paired-execution trial and evidence verifier would be required to make any later trading decision. No future profit is guaranteed.
