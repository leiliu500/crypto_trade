The next rebuild should evaluate a small, declared family of persistent inventory policies through one causal selector and one execution/accounting kernel. Neither an additional confidence threshold nor a larger cap addresses the complete problem. This audit inspected source and existing reports; it did not run a new strategy or inspect reserved 2026 outcomes.

Production ownership is a concrete integration issue. `src/engine/trading-engine.ts` constructs `DistributionController` when distribution mode is enabled, routes quote events through it, and requires a matching distribution decision again at submission. `src/profit/*` remains historical research. `src/portfolio/live-main.ts` is a separate shadow runner with its own preflight, rather than the active paper owner. A research selector needs an explicit, exclusive paper ownership adapter; changing its backtest acceptance does not change the running entry path.

Current inactivity has several distinct causes:

- Distribution admission is structurally sparse: separate asset/action banks, 12-dimensional local support, 48 samples, 32 effective samples, 3 or 7 weighted observed days, a fresh-support requirement, zero-centered prior weight 16, a 2.58-standard-error deduction, a tail penalty, and the worst execution scenario. These are declared research choices, not independently calibrated institutional requirements. The model itself describes its confidence calculation as an approximate robustness score. Keep its recorded failures intact; do not silently weaken those gates.
- `src/main.ts` deliberately separates the new risk-bounded sizing policy from old $12 training evidence. It logs `COLLECT_FRESH_RISK_BOUNDED_OUTCOMES` rather than importing incompatible fixed-size labels. That prevents mislabeled economic evidence, but it also creates a startup support dependency. Hourly bars can warm price features and a slower policy selector; they cannot recreate historical order-book fills for this microstructure model.
- `sizeDistributionContext` reserves the same quantity for both directions and every action, using the smaller best-side quantity and widest declared stop. The 1% participation limit can leave this quantity below the lot minimum despite deeper selected-side liquidity. A future selected-side planner can use cumulative depth inside its limit collar, but it needs matching shadow outcomes and a new execution identity.
- Historical portfolio v1/v2 retains a $12 cap, split asset targets, and a continuous governor `12 * min(remaining drawdown fraction, remaining daily-loss fraction)`. Combining fractional targets and a shrinking dollar cap can produce permanently infeasible lots before a formal hard loss breach. This is a structural feasibility failure in those old research policies, not evidence that the deployed cap remains $12. The current risk-bounded distribution cap is a separate policy.
- Weekly profit v1 produced zero orders in all eight saved runs because its conservative mean did not clear the full cost hurdle. Its gross-mean v2 relaxed that distinct hypothesis through variance-scaled allocation, generated entries, and still lost in all eight runs. Base results were approximately -$274 in 2024 and -$262 in 2025 H1. Entry scarcity and predictive loss are therefore separate problems. Replacing confidence with utility did not establish edge.
- Mandatory positive P&L and minimum exposure for both assets can be unsuitable for a one-slot selector that intentionally chooses one asset or cash. Old portfolio gates also combine very small dollar drawdown budgets with a $12 study notional. A new protocol should express account risk consistently and validate the chosen portfolio, while reporting each asset. This is a new declared objective; old failed gates and outcomes remain unchanged.

A bounded next registry could contain the ten single-asset policies formed by BTC/ETH times momentum lookbacks of 7, 30 and 90 completed days and reversal lookbacks of 1 and 3 completed days, plus cash. These are a proposed finite research family, not claimed profitable parameters. Do not expand the family after viewing results. Keep constant BTC and ETH exposures as reported comparators. If the research owner prefers another horizon family following the separate economic audit, freeze exactly one final registry before any new performance calculation and record its relationship to earlier failed hypotheses.

Each expert should emit a deterministic signed target at the same daily availability time, with a declared signal horizon and persistent inventory semantics. A common volatility/risk rule determines dollars; signal disagreement or volatility must not be applied as repeated independent shrinkage factors. Use the venue lot as the executable unit, reserve remaining loss headroom in dollars, and report the binding constraint when no lot fits. Do not force an order, round above a risk limit, or reset accumulated losses. Stop behavior must belong to the frozen expert specification. A stop cannot be followed by a same-signal automatic retry; require a subsequent valid decision. Avoid automatic hourly reductions for insignificant target drift by using one declared adjustment schedule/deadband while retaining emergency risk reductions.

The minimum selector contract can be a monthly cost-penalized follow-the-leader rule, rather than another return-regression model. For expert j and completed UTC week w, retain independently simulated liquidation equity E[j,w] and exact net log growth `g[j,w] = log(E[j,w] / E[j,w-1])`. Every E includes fees, funding, price marks and actual modeled inventory. Keep all observed losses and missing-accounting states; do not clip reported economic results. Admit a weekly observation only after its full period, fill/funding evidence and declared publication delay have completed. Cash has zero growth. Nonpositive or unknown equity makes an expert unavailable and remains a recorded failure.

At the monthly selection time t, use at most the previous 52 completed weeks, with 26 required for the initial selection. Let H[t] be the known number of weeks until the next selection and let C[j,t] be the executable cost of changing the actual current inventory to expert j's current target. An explicit ranking is `score[j,t] = H[t] * mean(g[j,w]) + log(1 - C[j,t] / E[current,t])`. Choose the largest valid score; compare cash with its actual closing cost, and prefer the incumbent on an exact tie. Unknown costs make the transition unavailable. This is a transparent empirical allocation rule, not a calibrated forecast, an optimal policy theorem, or proof of future profit. It adds no confidence gate whose practical effect is determined only after seeing entries.

Maintain two separate accounting objects: a continuously simulated account for every expert, and the actual selector account. The selector's P&L comes only from its own orders, partial fills, inventory, fees, funding and risk state. It must never be the sum of each month's hindsight-winning expert return or a splice of expert equity paths. Switching costs and persistent state change the problem materially; ordinary expert-regret intuition does not establish this trading system's profitability. [Cesa-Bianchi, Dekel and Shamir, Online Learning with Switching Costs](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/CesaBianchiDeSh13.pdf).

Use nested chronological evaluation. Seed experts from verified pre-2024 data. At each outer quarter in 2024 and 2025 H1, the selector may use only inner weekly records available before each of its monthly decisions. Any trainable expert parameter must be fixed beforehand or fitted exclusively inside that same earlier window. Purge forward labels whose payoff interval or publication time overlaps a decision boundary; an embargo is needed when labels remain unsettled, not as a substitute for event-time checking. Keep outer portfolio inventory and account risk continuous across the monthly selections. Report separate period summaries without resetting losses inside a period. Future observations must never influence earlier model selection, normalization, hashes or eligibility. [Bürkner, Gabry and Vehtari, Leave-Future-Out Cross-Validation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.06281).

2024 and 2025 H1 remain reused research data even after adopting nested folds. Report every expert and the selector, full calendar weeks including cash, net P&L, drawdown, turnover, funding coverage, fill availability, stop counts and loss concentration. Bootstrap the selector's already generated weekly path; do not bootstrap the retrospectively best expert and call it selected-policy evidence. Preserve a complete research-trial registry and avoid interpreting a nominal confidence bound as adjusted for the prior search. [Bailey et al., The Probability of Backtest Overfitting](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf).

Seal registry, selector, execution/risk rules, fees, funding interpretations, code hashes, data hashes and final acceptance criteria before calculating new outcomes. Only after the entire candidate and deployment adapter are frozen should January–July 2026 be opened once as the reserved evaluation. Any response to its result creates a new hypothesis and consumes its holdout status. A live bootstrap containing those dates must therefore wait for that freeze; silently reading them to initialize the model would also consume the reservation.

The active paper contract should expose `MarketHistoryCheckpoint`, `ExpertEvidenceCheckpoint`, `SelectorDecision`, `TargetIntent`, and the existing exact fill/funding ledger. A decision carries all availability timestamps, selected expert, complete source hashes and binding sizing reasons. Download verified closed bars and reconstruct the causal expert/selector history before startup; do not require seven new live calendar days for a bar-based policy. Preserve incomplete bars and missing funding as unknown. A fresh quote then instantiates the frozen target through the same planner used by the recorded-book replay: selected-side depth, inward price collar, actual lot/tick rules, delayed IOC arrival, partial fills, canceled remainders, and reductions confirmed before additions. A book gap invalidates executable readiness while leaving the historical model checkpoint intact.

Historical candles provide an economic screen. They do not establish volume availability at the opening instant, queue position, depth, partial fills or measured slippage. Run the identical target/position kernel on independently recorded books and on the paper broker, reconcile every state transition and net cash component, and continue prospective evidence collection. Promotion must be based on the selector portfolio's after-cost evidence and declared account limits. More entries, positive standalone expert history, a theoretical online-learning bound, or a warm historical model alone is insufficient to call the change profitable.
