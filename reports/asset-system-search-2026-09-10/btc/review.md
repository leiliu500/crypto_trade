The first method applies established linear state-estimation mathematics. Its two-dimensional state and propagated covariance differ from the earlier fixed-window SMA rule, but a constant-gain Kalman filter can also be represented as an infinite linear filter. This is not a claim of a new economic source of return or original mathematics. The source is [Kalman’s 1960 paper](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.3662552); its bibliographic record was verified, while direct paper retrieval failed in this session.

The Gaussian process uses the joint training-kernel inverse and posterior covariance from the standard regression construction. Its nonlinear kernel avoids the linear-kernel equivalence with ridge regression. It also differs from the repository’s independent Gaussian-distance weighted empirical averages: correlations among training points affect the solution, and coefficients need not be positive normalized weights. The fixed model-based uncertainty penalty is **not a calibrated financial prediction interval**. Fourteen-day labels overlap; the observation-noise model does not establish independence. [Rasmussen and Williams, Chapter 2](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW2.pdf).

The rank policy combines a descriptive Kendall statistic with the median of pairwise log-price slopes. A strictly increasing transformation preserves the rank component, whereas an arithmetic-average price rule generally changes; the slope gate retains sensitivity to economic return magnitude. These are established robust estimators, combined here into a particular policy. No p-value or test significance is asserted for serially dependent financial prices. Primary references are [Mann (1945)](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1907187) and [Sen (1968)](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1968.10480934); full-text access was unavailable, as recorded in the registry.

The directional-change policy measures changes with an event clock. An extremum can survive beyond any specified fixed lookback until the opposite event occurs. This differs from a finite calendar-window channel, but it remains closely related to familiar trailing-extrema controls. The conservative registry therefore **does not certify it as new mathematics or a new independent economic mechanism**. Published intrinsic-time work supplies the event-time lineage; it does not establish this daily BTC strategy’s profitability. [Glattfelder and Golub, Bridging the Gap](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02682).
