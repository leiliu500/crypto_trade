This initial catalog identifies **10 trading-system mechanisms in nine economic parent groups**. Four already exist in this repository. Six would require substantially new systems; two of those share the broad liquidity-service source of return. These are established mechanisms and proposed applications of known mathematics, **not 10,000 completed systems, original mathematical inventions, or validated profitable strategies**.

A system is distinct here when its economic payment source, contractual payoff, or state and action structure changes substantially. Different parameters, indicators, fitting algorithms, timeframes, symbols, or combinations of entry and exit rules do not increase the count. Cross-venue and triangular conversion routes are grouped under price parity. CLOB market making and AMM liquidity are separate system architectures but explicitly share a liquidity-service parent.

The full records in [economic-systems.json](economic-systems.json) include each source of expected return, who pays it, the mathematical control model, an illustrative equation, state and actions, public and private data requirements, repository overlap, costs, execution constraints, a first falsification test and verified primary sources. Whether “new” means established systems new to this engine or original inventions remains unresolved.

EC01's distinction from the existing engine is the continuous management of **both quotes and resulting inventory**, rather than placing a passive order for an already-selected directional trade. [Avellaneda and Stoikov](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/HighFrequencyTrading.pdf) supply a canonical stochastic-control model. The repository already has [queue-aware entry shadowing](../../src/execution/entry-route-shadow.ts) and [maker/taker routing](../../src/execution/hybrid-entry-router.ts), so those components are not being claimed as new. At published 40-basis-point spot maker fees on each side, a completed maker round trip needs approximately **80.32 basis points** of buy-to-sell appreciation just to cover commissions. This is a fee-feasibility constraint, not evidence that a BTC spread of that size is executable. Account-specific fees remain unverified. [Kraken fee schedule](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule).

EC02, EC03 and EC04 have different cash-flow mechanisms. Cross-market parity monetizes an already-observed price discrepancy but faces capital recycling and non-atomic execution. [Makarov and Schoar](https://www.fmg.ac.uk/sites/default/files/publications/DP782.pdf) document historical crypto segmentation, not a current opportunity. Dated carry has a specified settlement event; [BIS research](https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry) explains how leveraged demand and limited arbitrage capital can sustain its basis. Perpetual carry instead depends on future funding transfers and a changing exit basis. [Kraken's contract documentation](https://support.kraken.com/articles/4844359082772-linear-multi-collateral-derivatives-contract-specifications) describes continuous funding accrual with settlement at hour end or a net-position change. Existing [carry economics](../../src/carry/economics.ts) and [funding accrual](../../src/carry/funding-accrual.ts) already cover these latter mechanisms.

EC05 and EC06 do not have contractual payers. A directional forecast must establish predictive information that survives costs. [Time Series Momentum](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Journal-Article/Time-Series-Momentum) documents historical behavior in traditional instruments; it does not validate this crypto engine. [Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402) motivate studying order flow, but contemporaneous price association is insufficient evidence of future executable return. Changing a model architecture while retaining the same information, action and return source is not a new economic system.

EC07 sells protection and therefore bears adverse outcomes; option premium collected is not profit. The [Cboe volatility-risk-premia methodology](https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/governance/SVRPO_Methodology.pdf) is a structural reference, not evidence that crypto option insurance is profitable. EC08 searches for **contractual payoff inequalities**, independent of a volatility forecast. [Deribit's payoff explanation](https://insights.deribit.com/market-research/deribit-position-builder-primer-on-basic-option-strategies/) provides the parity foundation. A correct implementation must normalize [instrument currencies, settlement and quantity conventions](https://docs.deribit.com/api-reference/market-data/public-get_instrument), apply [trading and delivery costs](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944746248989-Fees), and prove payoff inequalities over exact breakpoints and tails. A sampled payoff grid or attractive midpoint quote is insufficient.

EC09 owns an automated reserve curve rather than a queue position. [Uniswap v3's whitepaper](https://app.uniswap.org/whitepaper-v3.pdf) describes that geometry; [current fee documentation](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/get-started/concepts/fees) identifies protocol and LP cash-flow rules. Fees must exceed costs relative to a matched benchmark, including [loss-versus-rebalancing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.06046). EC10 is a different protocol service: the liquidator repays eligible debt and receives collateral with a bonus. [Aave's documentation](https://aave.com/help/borrowing/liquidations) also describes intense execution competition. The catalog assumes already-funded capital and does not use borrowing or submit transactions.

For **new-to-engine research**, the first three useful investigations are EC02's synchronized funded-price-parity screen, EC08's exact executable-payoff screen, and EC01's fee-qualified inventory market-making shadow. This order reflects how directly each hypothesis can be falsified with observable prices and liabilities; it is not a ranking of demonstrated returns. Reject each screen if feasible net surplus vanishes after costs or execution constraints. Options and additional venues need new public data adapters before even that screen is credible. On-chain systems need a much larger data and execution stack and are not shortcuts to profit.

Validation checked ten unique records, nine declared economic parents, all 15 referenced primary-source IDs, every required field, and 22 local evidence-file hashes. No new backtest or trading experiment was run. The two catalog files are the only files changed for this subtask; runtime, orders, accounts and deployments were not changed.
