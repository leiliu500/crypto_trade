This is an **initial taxonomy of 11 established forecast or inference mechanisms**, far below the requested 10,000 systems. It makes no claim of 10,000 completed designs, original mathematical inventions, implemented profitable systems, or a best new strategy. “Not located in inspected engine” is a limited source-audit finding; it does not prove absence from all repository history or establish originality in the literature.

The [catalog](forecast-methods.json) defines each mechanism with canonical equations, required observations, the proposed economic application, its nearest existing equivalent, duplicate exclusions and verified primary sources. It deliberately counts broad mechanisms rather than state counts, window lengths, feature subsets, neural architectures or mixtures of familiar components. A forecast mechanism is also only part of a complete trading system: executable prices, funded sizing, exits, costs and evaluation rules still matter.

**F01, a small hidden Markov model, and F02, Bayesian online changepoint detection, are the first feasible prototypes using the saved daily data.** The distinction is structural. F01 assumes a finite set of recurring hidden regimes whose transition probabilities affect the next observation. F02 assigns posterior probability to how recently a new segment began; its segment parameters are newly drawn rather than labels from a fixed recurring menu. Existing tree-based regimes classify feature snapshots, while existing discounted Bayesian regression ages every observation by a fixed factor. Neither mechanism currently supplies either latent-state recursion. These models could address the hypothesized use of stale relationships across changing regimes. That failure mechanism is plausible, and improvement remains to be tested; more complicated state inference could simply overfit sparse transitions.

The daily dataset has 720 completed bars per symbol. That permits small causal implementation and predictive checks, but it is weak evidence for stable latent regimes or profitability after 80-basis-point-per-side research fees. A first comparison should freeze one specification per mechanism, calculate filtered predictions using only then-available observations, assess predictive likelihood against its matched simple baseline, and then assess an independently specified funded net-return policy. Smoothing hidden states with future evaluation observations would invalidate the trading replay. The previous research dates remain inspected development evidence even under a new model.

Each source establishes the named method. Applying it to crypto profit prediction is our proposed hypothesis; these papers are not evidence that the proposed application makes money. In particular, a more accurate tail or fill model may be useful without predicting a positive return.

The audit excludes already implemented ridge and discounted Bayesian regression, empirical kernel conditional distributions, CART regimes, Student-t natural-gradient boosting, CUSUM, OU/AR(1), moving-average trend, breakout, pullback and rotation. It also conservatively excludes survival-model branding because the execution planner already contains competing exponential fill/cancel hazards; richer fitted hazards would be an extension requiring a more specific novelty claim. Recursive variance models are likewise not counted because they sit close to existing EWMA variance recursions. A named Kalman or particle algorithm alone is not a new economic model, and neural architectural changes are grouped instead of inflated into separate inventions.

Hawkes and queue models require synchronized event/book records that daily OHLC cannot reconstruct. Broad latent-factor forecasting needs a historically available multi-series panel. Invariant causal prediction needs a defensible environment or intervention design; merely splitting the chart into dates does not supply one. Neural sequence and flexible flow-density models require substantially more independent training information. The catalog records these data barriers rather than treating every method as immediately implementable.

An honest 10,000-system count would require an explicit equivalence rule, a substantive causal/generative or inference distinction for every counted design, a complete specification, and independent evidence that the designs are not merely renamed or parameterized versions of each other. This initial list demonstrates useful alternatives and excludes duplicates; it does not establish that 10,000 materially different, relevant systems can be supplied or that any such count would identify a profitable winner. If the intended requirement is original mathematics, these established methods do not satisfy it.

**One of these mechanisms now has an isolated research implementation.** [hmm.py](hmm.py) and its [frozen specification](hmm-spec.json) implement F01 as a three-state Gaussian HMM-inspired ETH trading rule. The specification was recorded before historical outcomes. It fits only the first 90 returns using 40 deterministic regularized EM iterations, then freezes parameters and updates the forward state posterior. A 14-day expected cumulative log-return proxy must exceed the fixed base round-trip log cost to enter; it exits when that proxy is nonpositive. This is an established-model application, not original mathematics or a calibrated expected-dollar guarantee.

The implementation passed 23 synthetic checks, including exhaustive enumeration of latent paths against the forward likelihood/posterior, online-versus-batch filtering, a multiday-expectation identity, synthetic entry/exit behavior, nine prefix-invariance checks and unchanged inputs. Run `python3 reports/structural-system-search-2026-09-10/hmm.py --self-test` to reproduce them. No historical performance was evaluated by this author at this point; root owns the separate frozen market replay and validator audit. The author-level counts are **11 cataloged mechanisms, one implemented prototype, one prototype checked on synthetic inputs, zero historical performance evaluations, and zero validated profitable systems**. The 10,000-system request remains unmet.

Root subsequently completed the frozen historical replay. The [HMM diagnostic](hmm-diagnostic.md) finds that all 630 targets after warmup are ETH, and every one of 16 window/scenario ledgers exactly equals costed ETH buy-and-hold. The minimum forecast over every possible filtered posterior exceeds the entry hurdle, so the exit rule cannot fire under this frozen fit. Continuous net is −$296.88 base and −$330.71 under cost plus delay. This specific prototype therefore contributes no new effective trading behavior and no profitable replacement. The inference mathematics differs from prior code; the resulting policy does not differ from the passive control. No model retuning followed these outcomes.
