All runs start with **$10,000 cash**, one funded long position at most, and a **fee-inclusive entry limit of $1,000 and 10% of then-current equity**. No leverage or additions are modeled. Inventory appreciation can increase exposure above the entry ceiling. There is no enforced drawdown stop, and sampled daily-low drawdown is not a bound on actual intraday loss. Base costs are 80-basis-point fees plus 3-basis-point adverse prices per side. Combined stress uses 100 and 10 respectively and an extra daily bar of delay. Same-delay cost stress and separate delay stress are also retained. These are OHLC fill proxies, not observed executable profits, and exclude taxes and infrastructure costs. The current public fee schedule supports the entry-tier scenario, but the actual account tier remains unverified. [Kraken fee schedule](https://www.kraken.com/features/fee-schedule).

The **Bayesian changepoint prototype** maintains a posterior distribution over the length of the current regime. Each possible regime uses a conjugate normal-inverse-gamma return model, and its predictive likelihood updates the weights of continued regimes and resets. The full run-length bank is retained. This differs from a fixed moving-average window or a CUSUM threshold. The source method establishes an inference algorithm, not a trading profit theorem. [Adams and MacKay, Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742).

The trading rule uses a fixed 60-day expected regime duration, a 20-day survival-weighted drift projection and a one-standard-deviation penalty for uncertainty in the latent mean. It enters when that penalized projection clears the fixed base round-trip cost and expected run length is at least ten days; it exits when projected drift is nonpositive. This penalty is not a calibrated predictive loss quantile. The first 90 returns determine only prior variance, and later observations update the filter causally. It made **no trades in development or in the 2026 reset window**. Its two trades and losses occurred in second-half 2025. Its zero development utility selected it ahead of the losing HMM, but inactivity is not a profit-generating result. [Code](changepoint.py), [frozen protocol](prototype-screen/protocol.json).

The **hidden Markov prototype** models three recurring latent return states with Gaussian observations and a transition matrix. It fits one deterministic regularized model to the first 90 returns, freezes its parameters, and then updates only the forward state probabilities. It enters when expected 14-day log growth exceeds the fixed cost hurdle, exits at nonpositive projected growth, and holds between thresholds. Training-window forward/backward calculations use only observations available before the subsequent test; later decisions never use future-state smoothing. This is an application of established HMM mathematics. [Rabiner's HMM tutorial](https://web.ece.ucsb.edu/Faculty/Rabiner/ece259/Reprints/tutorial%20on%20hmm%20and%20applications.pdf).

Independent analysis found that the HMM's 14-day projected log growth from each pure state is **2.9726%, 3.1805% and 5.8307%**, all above its **1.6600% log-cost entry hurdle**. Any posterior is a convex mixture of those states, so its forecast must also clear the hurdle. The exit is unreachable under the frozen fit, regardless of future observations. All 630 targets after warmup hold ETH, and all 16 window/scenario ledgers equal passive ETH exactly. Therefore this tranche has **two implemented inference models but only one nonbaseline fitted trading policy**. No model was retuned after this finding. [Proof and diagnostic](hmm-diagnostic.md).

The prior ETH40 control remains the strongest historical result **among these compared implementations**, not the best system in a 10,000-system search or a demonstrated future profit solution. Its earlier screen still fails the episode and drawdown requirements, its uncertainty bounds remain negative, and its gains depend on a few large episodes. Those failed checks remain in the [prior study](../profit-search-100-2026-09-10/report.md). The new results reject these two particular prototypes; they do not prove that every HMM or changepoint application must fail.

These are **provisional established-method families**, not eleven original inventions or eleven fully integrated trading systems. Their equations, economic hypotheses, required observations, nearest equivalents and source evidence are recorded. A forecast mechanism can change without creating a new economic source of return.

The [economic-system catalog](economic-systems.json) examines two-sided inventory market making, funded multi-market price parity, dated carry, perpetual funding carry, directional persistence, order-flow prediction, option insurance premia, exact derivative payoff dominance, AMM liquidity provision and liquidation services. Four already exist in the repository and are excluded from new-system counts. Six require materially new systems; CLOB and AMM liquidity share the same broad liquidity-service parent. These architectures need different data and cash-flow accounting. They cannot all be tested credibly with the current daily long/cash replay.

The additional [price-parity observation](parity-snapshot.md) checks displayed BTC/USD and ETH/USD prices at Kraken and Coinbase Exchange. It computes the maximum cost budget available from buying the displayed ask at one venue and selling already-owned inventory at the other's bid. This tests an observable-price discrepancy rather than a directional forecast. The collector retains raw responses, receipt timing, cache evidence, failed requests and unknown quote ages. It neither establishes simultaneous fills nor places orders; actual account fees and executable net profit remain unknown. The separate report is the authority for the captured sample and its availability.

The completed sample contains **12 directional comparisons across three rounds in 8.222 seconds**. Six had positive displayed gross gaps, but the largest allowed only **0.5266 basis points total for all costs**. A diagnostic using the public Kraken entry-tier 80-basis-point taker charge on its leg alone, while assuming zero Coinbase fee and no other costs, made every comparison negative; the best was **-79.4776 basis points of buy notional**. That cost-scale diagnostic was added after observing the quotes and is explicitly not a preselected strategy test. Cached responses, uncertain Kraken snapshot age and one Coinbase book older than the diagnostic limit further prevent an executable-opportunity claim. The initial sandbox DNS failures and the nanosecond-timestamp parsing failure are preserved; the successful raw capture was reanalyzed offline after the parser correction.

The next useful structural research priorities are **funded price parity, exact contractual payoff dominance, and inventory-aware market making subject to a fee-feasibility check**. This ordering reflects how directly their surplus can be measured and falsified, not demonstrated profitability. A payoff optimizer needs matching contracts, exact settlement conventions and executable combination prices. A market maker needs both-side inventory accounting, queue and cancellation events, adverse-selection measurements and account-specific fees. The canonical quoting-control mathematics does not establish that current spreads exceed those costs. [Avellaneda and Stoikov](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/HighFrequencyTrading.pdf).

The counting rule is now explicit: parameter values, asset substitutions, algebraic rewrites, renamed code, fixed blends and Cartesian-product packaging do not create additional mathematical systems. Different observed paths are neither necessary nor sufficient proof of structural novelty. A count of original inventions would additionally require derivations and literature-equivalence review. Structural deduplication must preserve every tried variant in the statistical search ledger; it cannot erase selection bias.
